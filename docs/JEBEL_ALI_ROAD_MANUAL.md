# Jebel Ali Road Manual

> **Accuracy notice.** Coordinates, exit/gate/roundabout numbers, lane counts and station details in this project are approximate or schematic. They were compiled as a navigation aid and have not been surveyed. Road signs and RTA instructions always override this app. Please correct `database/dubai-roads.json` as you verify items on the road.

## Corridors
- **E11 Sheikh Zayed Road** — coast highway from the Abu Dhabi border through JAFZA, Ibn Battuta, Marina, Mall of the Emirates and Downtown. Exits are on the right; plan lane changes before the gantry.
- **E311 Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road** — inland bypass past Jebel Ali Industrial, DIP, Expo City, Global Village and Mirdif. Heavy trucks use the right lanes.
- **E611 Emirates Road** — freight and bypass corridor towards Al Maktoum Airport.
- **E77 Expo Road** — links JAFZA gates with Expo City, DIP, E311/E611 and Al Maktoum Airport.
- **D57 Al Yalayis**, **D59 Garn Al Sabkha**, **D53**, **D63**, **D54**, **E44 Al Khail**, **E66 Dubai–Al Ain** — local arteries.

## Rules of thumb
1. Choose your lane at the *first* gantry. Solid white lines must not be crossed.
2. Missed an exit? Never reverse or cross the gore area. Use the **Missed your exit?** card to find the next legal U-turn.
3. U-turn types: **Loop** (free-flow), **Signal** (wait for the arrow, far-left lane), **R/A** (give way inside the roundabout).
4. Free-right slip lanes still require you to yield and are often camera-monitored.
5. **Speed**: the posted limit is the legal limit. Dubai radars are widely reported to flash around 20 km/h above it, and that tolerance should not be relied on; it does not apply in Abu Dhabi.
6. **Salik** (variable tariff, Dubai time): AED 6 peak (06:00–10:00 and 16:00–20:00, Mon–Sat), AED 4 off-peak (10:00–16:00, 20:00–01:00, and all day Sunday), free 01:00–06:00. Verify at salik.ae.
7. **Heavy vehicles**: RTA publishes truck-movement bans; the in-app hours are indicative only.

## JAFZA quick guide
- Gates 1–6 (North) and 7–12 (South) are modelled as a schematic ring; roundabouts R/A 1–15 form a 3 × 5 grid. Confirm numbers with the JAFZA gate maps.
- Cargo gates are shown in amber. Port terminals T1–T4 require port passes and customs documents.
- Fuel: stations at JAFZA North/South, the Port gate and Industrial 1–2 serve trucks; see the Gas filter's amenity toggles.
